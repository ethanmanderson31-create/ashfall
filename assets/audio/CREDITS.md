# Audio credits

All recorded sounds are CC0 (public domain) from OpenGameArt.org:

- **RPG Sound Pack** by artisticdude: creature (guttural beast, giant, ogre), battle swings, inventory and interface sounds.
- **80 CC0 creature SFX** by rubberduck: barks, howl, roars.
- **Monster Sound Effects Pack** (CC0): boss layers.

Everything in `synth/` is synthesized for Ashfall by `tools/sfx_synth.py`.

Added (all CC0, OpenGameArt.org):

- **The Free Firearm Sound Library** (rifle shots, sliced into single shots by `tools/slice_shots.py`)
- **Gun reload sounds** (assault rifle and rifle reloads)
- **150 Combat Sound Effects** compilation (Kenney Impact Sounds punches, explosions)
- **Impact** by qubodup (meat and stone impacts)
- **37 hits/punches** by Independent.nu (crunch hits)
- **Monster Sound Effects 2** (attack grunts), **Monsters or Beasts** (growls), **Monster snarls** by Darsycho
